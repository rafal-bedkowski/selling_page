Privacy & Cookies Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what data drivetheproduct.pm ("this site") collects, why, and what cookies it uses. This site is run by Rafał Będkowski as a personal project. If you have questions, contact drivetheproduct@gmail.com.

What data is collected

If you submit the free PDF form or subscribe to the newsletter, the following is collected:

  • Your first name (freebie form only)
  • Your email address

This data is used only to send you the requested file and/or the newsletter you signed up for. It is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data: Formspree, Kit, Google.

Cookies

This site itself does not run analytics or advertising cookies. The only cookies set are by the embedded Kit newsletter form, which uses cookies to operate the signup widget and prevent spam submissions. When you first visit, you can Accept or Decline the cookie notice — this choice is remembered in your browser (using local storage) so the banner does not reappear on every visit.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the data associated with your email address at any time by emailing drivetheproduct@gmail.com. Every newsletter email also includes an unsubscribe link that removes you from the list immediately.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site or the tools behind it change. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest version.
